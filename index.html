Recreating vintage camera looks

A short companion to this repo of readable Python implementations. These are the same retro photo effects that power DigicamFilter, a free in-browser tool that runs locally and never uploads your photos.

Favorite presets

  • CCD filter — the cool, slightly green early-digicam color.
  • iPhone 4 filter — that specific 2010 phone-camera look.
  • Disposable camera filter — hard flash and heavy grain.
  • 35mm film filter — fine grain and gentle highlight roll-off.
  • Vintage camera filter — faded, warm, general "a while ago" look.

The idea: half of "retro" aesthetics are just old camera limitations switched back on by choice. A flawless photo looks like an ad; a slightly broken one looks like a memory. More at digicamfilter.online.
